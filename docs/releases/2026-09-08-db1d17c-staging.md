## Recovery and remaining gates

`li-os-release-a7601b7` remains a schema-compatible availability fallback but contains the
transport-log privacy defect corrected by `db1d17c`. It must not be described as privacy-safe or
used for Calendar/provider reads merely to prove health. If an incident makes fallback unavoidable,
record the restored risk, minimize provider activity and prepare a corrected immutable release.
Application rollback does not reverse schema 0.42.

The next Calendar step needs separate exact authorization for at most one bounded read. Inspect only
the new `li.calendar` action, category, stage and validated status fields for the corresponding
request—not event data, raw responses, queries, credentials or arbitrary historical logs. Stop after
the first outcome; any OAuth, scope, API, calendar-ID or provider-setting repair is another protected
action. Provider-backed Calendar display, physical-device/owner/stability acceptance, historical-log
assessment and the action-time-confirmed `LIOS42` archive remain outstanding. Weekly Avanza quotes
and specialist recency ordering remain planned rather than implemented.
