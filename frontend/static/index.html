    Voice & Privacy

    Voice starts only when you press the microphone and your browser grants permission. Audio is processed ephemerally by the browser speech capability and is not uploaded, logged, stored in memory, analytics, artifacts, or conversation history by Li. Only the visible transcript is handled like a normal chat message. Availability, quality, and language voices vary by browser and device.
