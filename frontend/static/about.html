Private personal workspace

One trusted place to work with Li.

Li OS is a private AI workspace operated for one owner. It brings conversations, specialist support, personal planning, Calendar and carefully governed actions into one secure interface.

Private by design

Access is restricted to the approved owner account. Credentials remain on the server, not in browser code.

Useful with boundaries

Li can read permitted information and prepare actions, while consequential writes retain explicit approval controls.

Clear about outcomes

Unavailable providers and uncertain results are reported honestly rather than presented as completed work.

Google Calendar

Li OS uses Google Calendar access to show the owner’s schedule and to create an event only after an explicit approval. Calendar data is handled through Li’s private backend and is not used for advertising or sold.

Read the Privacy Policy for the exact data handling and revocation details. Use of this private deployment is also covered by the Terms of Service.
